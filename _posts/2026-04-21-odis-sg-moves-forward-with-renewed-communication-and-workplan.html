--- title: "ODIS-SG Moves Forward with Renewed Communication and Workplan" date: 2026-04-21 author: "Stephen Formel" ---

On 21 April 2026, the ODIS Steering Group met with a focus on reactivating community engagement and making progress on the 2026–2028 workplan ahead of the IODE Committee meeting in October 2026. The group agreed to send a narrative communication to National Oceanographic Data Centres (NODCs) and Associated Data Units (ADUs) confirming that ODIS remains a priority, and noted that while the ODIS search interface has been offline, no data has been lost thanks to ODIS's federated approach, with a proof-of-concept for a restored interface already in development. Discussions also touched on progress toward the IOC Data Architecture, including new Minimum Viable Products and a prototype Clearinghouse Mechanism for the BBNJ Treaty, as well as continued support for NODCs and ADUs through the Quality Management Framework and mentoring initiatives.
