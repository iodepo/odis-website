--- title: "ODIS is welcoming a new collaboration with Universal Plastic" date: 2025-09-29 author: "Sofie De Baenst" ---

Universal Plastic has officially joined the Ocean Data and Information System (ODIS). This partnership ensures that plastic cleanup data collected through Universal Plastic’s app becomes visible to IOC member states and the global ocean community, strengthening efforts to regenerate marine ecosystems and advance the UN Decade of Ocean Science for Sustainable Development.
