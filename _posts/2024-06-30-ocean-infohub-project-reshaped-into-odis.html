--- title: "Ocean InfoHub Project Reshaped into ODIS" date: 2024-06-30 author: "Stephen Formel" ---

Ocean InfoHub Project has ended in June 2024 and was reshaped into the IODE Programme Component: ODIS: Ocean Data and Information System.

On 16 May 2024 the final Ocean Infohub meeting took place (online) in 2 different time zones, so that all regional partners could attend. During this meeting the progress (since the SG-IV meeting in October 2023) was discussed, together with a overview on the transition to come of the Ocean Infohub Project to the Ocean Data and Information System as a IODE Programme component. All 3 pilot regions gave an overview on the achievements, the challenges and activities to come. The final meeting report can be found on OceanExpert: https://oceanexpert.org/document/37533
